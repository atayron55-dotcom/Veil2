WELCOME TO THE VEIL

Private channels, direct to the edge.

Create a permanent link once and share it with someone you trust. Veil keeps every conversation alive in its own encrypted peer-to-peer tunnel, reconnects automatically, and never stores your messages on a server.

Configure the rendezvous service in Settings to get started.

Your conversations never leave your devices.

The rendezvous service only relays the technical handshake needed to open a direct connection. It never receives your messages, calls, photos or files.
